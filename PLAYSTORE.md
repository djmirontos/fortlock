## Full Description (4000 characters max)

FortLock is a privacy-first password manager and 2FA authenticator built for people who take security seriously. Your passwords never leave your device — everything is encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM, the same standard used by banks and governments.

🔐 MILITARY-GRADE ENCRYPTION
Every credential is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a master key derived from your password via PBKDF2 with 310,000 iterations. Your master password is never stored — only you can unlock your vault.

📱 COMPLETE OFFLINE SUPPORT
FortLock works entirely offline. No account required, no cloud sync, no subscriptions. Your passwords stay on your device, always.

🔑 BUILT-IN 2FA AUTHENTICATOR
Generate time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) for all your accounts — Gmail, Microsoft, GitHub, Facebook, Instagram, and hundreds more. Scan QR codes or enter secret keys manually. Works completely offline.

🖥️ WEB BRIDGE — SEND TO DESKTOP
Securely send passwords from your phone to any desktop browser in seconds. Open fortlock-web.vercel.app, scan the QR code from your phone, and your password appears on your desktop. Sessions expire in 60 seconds and nothing is stored on the server.

🔒 BIOMETRIC UNLOCK
Unlock your vault instantly with your fingerprint or Face ID. Fast, secure, and convenient.

📦 FLEXIBLE BACKUP & EXPORT
Export your vault as an encrypted .flbx backup, a CSV file compatible with 1Password and Bitwarden, or a KeePass-compatible XML file. Your data is always yours.

✨ POWERFUL FEATURES
- Organize credentials by category — Banking, Social, Email, General
- Mark favorites for quick access
- Search and filter your vault instantly
- Sort by recent or alphabetical
- Auto-lock after configurable inactivity timer
- 30-second clipboard auto-clear
- Light, Dark, and System theme support
- Service logos automatically loaded for 100+ popular services

🛡️ WHAT WE DON'T DO
- We don't collect any data
- We don't require an account
- We don't have access to your passwords
- We don't show ads
- We don't charge subscriptions

FortLock is built for people who want real security without sacrificing usability. Whether you're storing passwords, banking cards, or 2FA codes — FortLock keeps everything safe, organized, and always within reach.
